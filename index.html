Hi, I'm Sreeram. I like taking a messy question, building something that can answer it, and then checking if the answer holds up.

I'm finishing a Master's in Data Science at UT Arlington. My project list doesn't sit still on purpose — a healthcare risk model, a churn classifier, a stock-forecasting LSTM, a full-stack tutoring platform, a gene-expression study, and right now, a controlled experiment on when retrieval-augmented generation actually helps a language model. Before any of that, I trained as an electronics engineer in India, which is probably why I still think in systems and signals before I think in dashboards.

The common thread isn't a tool or a title. It's the habit of not trusting a result until I've tried to break it. Off duty, I'm probably watching a movie — ideally in the best seat in the house.
